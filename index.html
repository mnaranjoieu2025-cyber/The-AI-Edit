01 / THE BASICS

First, what is AI?

AI is technology that can do things we normally need people for, like answering questions, finding patterns, or creating images.

It learns from huge amounts of data. Chatbots, for example, predict what words should come next. That's why they can sound so human, even when they're wrong.

Based on OECD.AI and ISO.

03 / WHAT'S NEXT?

So, where do
we go from here?

The possibilities

AI could make studying more personal and help scientists find answers faster. There’s a lot of potential, but nothing is guaranteed.

The reality check

AI needs a lot of electricity. The IEA expects data center electricity use to nearly double by 2030 compared with 2025. That could put pressure on power grids.

Still need humans

AI systems can be hacked or go offline. A 2025 Cloudflare outage disrupted major websites. That’s why people still need to check important decisions and have backup plans.

Sources: IEA energy outlook (2026) · Reuters on Cloudflare outage (2025).

I wanted this website to feel more like a mini digital magazine than a typical school assignment. I chose the name “The AI Edit” because my idea was to show two sides of AI: the things that make it useful and the risks we should think about. I divided the information into simple sections so it's easy to follow even if someone doesn't know much about technology.

For the design, I chose warm cream, chocolate brown, berry red, and dusty pink colors. I used Georgia for the headings and Arial for the smaller text. The large title and the two-color opening layout were inspired by the editorial websites I collected as references. I made the website using HTML for the content and CSS for the colors, spacing, and two-column comparisons and editorial images. The images were cropped from AI-assisted design mockups, and I checked the layout on different screen sizes. I also added simple links to move between sections and to open my sources.

I used ChatGPT to help research the topic, organize my ideas, and draft the HTML and CSS and explore image ideas. I reviewed the information against sources such as the OECD, NIST, IEA, and news reporting, and edited the writing to keep it simple. The final content and design choices are my responsibility.
